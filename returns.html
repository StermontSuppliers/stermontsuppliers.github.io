Returns and refunds

Last updated: October 2026

We want you to be happy with what you receive. If something is wrong with your order, we will make it right.

When you can return an item

  • The item arrived faulty or damaged.
  • The item is different from what you ordered.
  • Part of your order is missing.

How to report a problem

  1. Message us on WhatsApp within 48 hours of delivery.
  2. Send clear photos of the item, the packaging and the problem.
  3. Tell us your name, phone number and what you ordered.

We will review it and arrange a replacement or a refund. Refunds are sent by M-Pesa to the number you paid from or another number you give us.

Please note

  • Keep the item in its original packaging until we have replied.
  • For agrovet products such as seeds, chemicals, feeds and animal medicines, please do not open or use more than needed to check the product. Opened products cannot be returned unless they are faulty or wrong.
  • Items damaged by misuse, wrong storage or wrong use are not covered.
  • Some products come with a manufacturer warranty. Ask us on WhatsApp before you buy if this matters to you.

Changed your mind?

Message us as soon as possible, before the order is dispatched, and we will do our best to cancel or change it.
