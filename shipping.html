Shipping and delivery

Last updated: October 2026

We deliver agrovet supplies, electronics, musical and studio equipment, fashion and home goods to all 47 counties in Kenya.

How ordering works

  • Pick your items and send your order through the cart or on WhatsApp.
  • We confirm stock, the total and the delivery fee with you.
  • You pay by M-Pesa. Your order is processed once payment is received.
  • We deliver to your location.

Delivery time

Delivery is usually within 1 to 3 days. Remote areas can take longer. We confirm the delivery time for your location before you pay.

Delivery fee

The fee depends on your location and the size and weight of your items. We tell you the exact fee before you pay, so there are no surprises.

Payment

We accept M-Pesa. We will send you the payment details on WhatsApp. Please only pay to the details we send you in the chat, and check the name shown before you confirm.

What we need from you

  • Your name and phone number
  • Your county and town, with a landmark or description of the exact place
  • A phone number that will be answered on the day of delivery

When your order arrives

Please check the items when you receive them. If something is faulty, damaged or not what you ordered, see our returns page and message us within 48 hours with photos.

Collecting from the shop

You can also visit us at The Sarit Centre (Westlands), Karuna Road, Nairobi. Message us first so we can confirm your items are ready.
