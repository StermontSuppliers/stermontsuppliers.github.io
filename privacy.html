Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

This page explains what information we collect when you use stermontarcade.co.ke or order from us, and how we use it. We handle personal data in line with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019.

What we collect

  • Your name, phone number and delivery details when you place an order.
  • The items you ordered and the order total.
  • Messages you send us on WhatsApp, by email or through the contact form.
  • If you create an account, your login details.

Why we use it

  • To process, confirm and deliver your order.
  • To contact you about your order, payment, delivery or a return.
  • To answer your questions.
  • To keep records of orders, as the law requires.

We do not sell your information. We do not use it to send marketing messages unless you ask us to.

Who handles your information

We use trusted services to run the shop. Your order details may pass through these services:

  • Supabase, where we store orders and stock records.
  • Web3Forms, which emails us your order and contact messages.
  • WhatsApp, if you order or chat with us there.
  • M-Pesa, for payments. We never see your M-Pesa PIN.
  • Our delivery partners, who receive only what they need to deliver to you (name, phone number and address).

Cookies and local storage

The site keeps your cart and login on your own device using your browser’s storage, so your cart is still there when you come back. We do not use this to track you across other websites.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as needed to deliver your order, handle returns and meet legal and accounting requirements. After that we delete or anonymise them.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, and you can object to how we use it. Message us using the details below and we will respond as soon as we can.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no website or online service is completely secure. Please only share payment details through the channels we confirm in your chat.

Changes

We may update this page. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
